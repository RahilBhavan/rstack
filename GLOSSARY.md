# Language

- **Skill entry:** one canonical `SKILL.md` plus metadata pointing to its package.
- **Catalog:** a dated inventory of entries and discovery issues.
- **Pack:** a browsing category; membership does not enable a skill.
- **Workflow:** task-specific composition guidance, inputs, checks and stop rules.
- **Plan:** resolved workflow guidance, not execution or permission.
- **Managed source:** a plugin, harness or synced package maintained outside rstack.
- **Entry hash:** SHA-256 of `SKILL.md` only; supporting files are not covered.
- **Source evidence:** recorded provenance pointers, not verified authorship or licensing.

- **Vendored source:** an unmodified, version-pinned upstream snapshot with its
  original license, a recorded commit and per-file integrity hashes. Availability
  as source is distinct from a registered native command or runtime integration.
- **Reference-only draft:** a preserved upstream skill excluded from normal
  discovery, such as the Matt Pocock in-progress collection.
